# Privacy

**SochaGuard does not collect, send or share any data: everything stays encrypted on your device, and the app does not use the network.**

- No account, no analytics, no advertising, no crash reporting.
- No data is transmitted to the developer or any third party.
- Your vault lives in the app's data folder in your user profile (Windows) or in the app's private storage (Android). Exports are files you create and control.
- This website (GitHub) is operated by GitHub and has its own privacy policy; downloading releases is subject to it.

Questions: open an issue (never include secrets).
